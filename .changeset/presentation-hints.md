Presentation transitions: viewers whose system asks for reduced motion can play the transitions anyway on their device, and get a one-time hint when a presentation starts. The Transitions menu shows how many frames have their own transition and can use one transition for all frames. The "Zoom" transition is now called "Scale". A hint suggests a relay (TURN) server if a connection to another session does not come up.

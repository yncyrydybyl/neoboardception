Add presentation transitions on the infinite canvas: the camera flies between frames (zoom out, pan, zoom in), or the view fades, slides, zooms or jumps. The transition, its speed and per frame overrides are stored in the board, so everyone who follows the presentation sees the same transition. Add an overview of all frames (O or Escape), a progress indicator and more keyboard shortcuts (Home, End, Page Up/Down, Shift+Space). With reduced motion the viewer gets a fade.
